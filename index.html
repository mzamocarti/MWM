Clean Streets, Stronger Community

Our main aim at Mzamo Waste Management (MWM) is to keep streets clean while creating meaningful impact within the community. We achieve this through effective recycling practices that reduce waste, minimise landfill usage, and promote environmental sustainability.

We also provide opportunities for community members to earn an income by collecting recyclable materials and exchanging them for value through our system.

At MWM, we believe waste management goes beyond collection—it is about environmental stewardship and social upliftment. By turning waste into value, we are helping to create a sustainable cycle of growth that benefits both people and the environment.

In collaboration with a leading national recycling organisation recognised for its excellence in circular economy solutions and large-scale material recovery, we are able to strengthen the impact and efficiency of our recycling initiatives.
